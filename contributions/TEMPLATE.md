## Rules, by frequency

One entry per rule. The frequency counts messages that triggered the rule.

The **type** separates two things:
- a **reading rule** improves the text for any reader, whoever the writer is (define terms before using them, one name per concept, no references to drafts the reader never saw);
- a **style preference** or **process preference** reflects your taste or the way you like to work (avoiding "rather than", preferring "problem" to "task", marking edits in blue).

When unsure, ask: would a careful reader with different tastes still be helped by this rule? If yes, it is a reading rule.
