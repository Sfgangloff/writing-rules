# Figures, tables and LaTeX

- Keep captions short and descriptive, and say which experiment the figure or table reports. Remove details that do not serve the figure's purpose.
- One figure has one purpose. Put an illustration of a notion where that notion is defined.
- Use proper tables: rules where readability needs them, balanced column widths, and images aligned with their text.
- Format names consistently (do not mix `anti_knight` and `AntiKnight`).
- Check that the PDF compiles, that tables and figures do not overlap, and that long URLs in the bibliography do not overflow.
- Use the current year's style files for the venue.
