# Position papers

- State the thesis explicitly, early and consistently. When the thesis changes, re-center the whole paper on it (abstract, introduction, roadmap).
- **Alternative views** are usually mandatory. Present independent, existing positions that conflict with yours, with citations. Objections to your own paper are not alternative views.
- Include a call to action if the venue requires one, and check the current call for papers for other rules.
- Expect reviewers to ask for technical substance. Ground conceptual claims in practice and in the literature.
- An informal idea from the author is developed into an argument. Do not decorate it with artificial structure.
- On arXiv versions, drop the venue prefix from the title (for example "Position:").
